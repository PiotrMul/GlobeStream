FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does GlobeStream include channels?
GlobeStream includes a curated collection of M3U streams from around the world, organised by continent, region and country.
What is Editor Mode?
Editor Mode is an advanced feature for power users. It lets you generate M3U files compatible with siptv.app, assign EPG codes and share playlists. Requires Downloads access and email verification.
Is there an iOS version?
iOS support is planned. Follow the project on GitHub to stay updated.
What data does the app collect?
GlobeStream does not collect personal data. Only the email for optional Editor Mode activation is processed. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
